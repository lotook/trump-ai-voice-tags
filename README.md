# Trump AI Voice: Script and Delivery Tag Cheatsheet

Tips for writing short parody scripts for [Trump AI Voice](https://trumpaivoice.com/), a browser-based Donald Trump-style AI text-to-speech tool for memes, satire and entertainment.

## Inline delivery tags
The generator lets you steer delivery with simple tags inside the script:

| Tag | Effect | Example |
|---|---|---|
| laugh | adds a laugh | "Nobody does pancakes better. [laugh]" |
| shout | louder, emphatic line | "[shout] This is the best birthday ever!" |
| whisper | quiet aside | "[whisper] Don't tell anyone." |
| pause | a beat of silence | "Tremendous. [pause] Really tremendous." |

(Check the tool's editor for the exact tag syntax.)

## Writing scripts that sound right
- Keep sentences short and repetitive; the persona works best with punchy lines.
- Put the joke in the last three words of a sentence.
- Use a pause tag before the punchline.
- English gives the most natural result; Chinese, Spanish and Japanese also work.

## Ideas
Birthday greetings, stream intros, meme voice-overs, TikTok/Shorts/Reels skits, prank voicemails for friends.

## Responsible use
Label clips as parody, don't use them to mislead, and don't present generated audio as a real statement.
